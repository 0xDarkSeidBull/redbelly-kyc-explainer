Do I need KYC on Redbelly? (image attached)

Not needed: holding or trading RBNT on an exchange, or bridged RBNT on another chain (Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain and others).
Needed: anything on Redbelly mainnet. Sending, swapping, bridging in or out, staking, DAO voting.

Get verified: access.redbelly.network, then Connect wallet, Begin identity verification, Continue with Averer. You need MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet and a passport. The issuer covers the access fees.
Approval: usually a few minutes, rarely a few days.
Wallets: up to 10 per verified identity.
Region: the DAO is open to every region. Redbelly Network does not offer services in the 18 jurisdictions in clause 15.1 of its terms.
Stuck or rejected: open a ticket at averer-help.freshdesk.com/support/tickets/new

Full guide with sources: https://kyc.test-hub.xyz
